# R06 — Hostile images and QR payloads

**Status: NOT EXECUTED as a complete hardware procedure — T2B1 and T3B1.**

Use the [shared acceptance record](README.md), with a separate result for every
required model/OS. Host and emulator output remains separate evidence.

## Preconditions

R05 passed; test device; retained hashed test/fuzz corpus and mutations; monitored power.

## Steps

1. Run `FUZZ_SECONDS=60 make fuzz`, retain results and minimised crashes, then run `make test-c`.
2. Copy to the physical volume: truncated PNG/JPEG, bad PNG CRC, dimensions >1024, files >256 KiB, excessive expansion, progressive JPEG, other formats disguised as images, non-otpauth/FB1 QR and malformed URIs. Use `tests/c/fimg`, `tests/c/fqr`, `tests/c/fmsc` corpora and generators; record every transmitted hash.
3. Try two QR codes in one image, no QR, heavy noise, limit sizes/rotation and rewriting during a copy. Fragment/reorder sectors with the fmsc harness and compare against actual OS copies.
4. After each rejection, eject, inspect accounts/keys and perform FIDO and GET CHALLENGE. Import a valid control image after the hostile series.
5. Interrupt a copy and reconnect; verify no partial identity and no persistent startup volume.

## Expected result

Bounded explicit rejection; no content execution, panic/overflow or secret mutation without consent; recovery after errors. Host fuzzing does not replace MCU RAM/time measurements.

## Evidence to retain

Exact corpus, hashes/seeds, sanitizer/fuzz logs, observed per-model errors/times, before/after state and valid control-import result.
